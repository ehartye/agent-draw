# Noir craft retrospective

The owner asked what a stronger graphic-novel pass should focus on and asked that the lessons enrich the plugin. This is an original critique of the current artwork and supplied images. It does not change the game or claim that a second visual pass has been rendered.

## Inspected evidence

- Current scene: `docs/quality/noir-evidence/final-street.png`, with its source in `examples/noir/art.mjs`. Thin light contours describe the hero and cars; regular window grids and wet-road cuts repeat across the stage. The palette and motifs communicate noir, but the figure's broad masses and the shot's depth can carry more expression.
- [Owner-supplied coat image](https://frankmillerink.com/wp-content/uploads/1427134-frank-miller-wallpaper-1920x1080-for-phone.jpg): a large white coat mass against black, cut by black fold wedges, selected rough edges and a compressed head/shoulder gesture. Observed image features are reference prompts, not claims about the artist's working method.
- [Owner-supplied rooftop action](https://locustmoon.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/07/holyterror2.jpg): an enormous cropped foreground boot, a small distant figure and converging architecture establish depth and force. The difference in scale and crop matters beyond camera roll.
- Existing correction evidence: `docs/quality/noir-review.md` and `noir-evidence/` retain the stair crossbar ambiguity, corrected native landing art, source/image review and route/input acceptance. Those tested outcomes remain distinct from the proposals below.

## Priority for a visual trial

1. Replace uniform figure outlines with deliberate black/white masses, selected edge loss and a few expressive fold cuts. Keep the construction mannequin for anatomy checking beneath the finished ink.
2. Recompose one street shot with a large cropped foreground shape and a stronger depth/scale relationship. Preserve the movement plane and visible destinations; reserve extreme close-ups for inspection or transitions.
3. Strengthen a few whole-pose keys: support, compressed torso, shoulder/pelvis attitude, purposeful hands and wind-loaded coat. Retain opposing gait phases, coherent boots and fixed prop attachment.
4. Stage weather and surface marks into depth groups and quiet areas. Reduce uniform grids and evenly distributed detail. Check a still frame and actual motion, including reduced motion.
5. Compose captions and accent into the shot's focal rhythm. Review at the smallest actual viewport and with controller prompts visible.

The practical next trial is one hero key plus one street shot, with matching before/after native exports and actual engine captures. Broad mass/framing changes have not yet received that trial. No new shader, mask or engine API is justified by this critique: the current path/fill tools can express the initial experiment.

## Reusable plugin guidance

`skills/draw-authoring/references/graphic-noir.md` turns these observations into a construction sequence and acceptance checklist. The mandatory fundamentals reference links to it for high-contrast comic/noir work. Existing anatomy and materials references remain the authority for joint construction, boots, attachments and support-surface hierarchy. The additions are authoring guidance in the feature branch; they are not a new published marketplace version.
